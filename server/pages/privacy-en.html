Perixd privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Perixd is a period, symptom and contraception tracker for Android, made by Aallyn, an independent developer in Portugal. This policy covers the Perixd app and the Perixd sync server at perixd.mildhope.net. Perixd has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and never sells or shares your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's private storage on your device: the days you bleed and how heavily, symptoms and their intensity, notes, the doses you mark as taken, and your settings (contraceptive method, pack or cycle start, reminder times). It is excluded from Android cloud backups. Predictions are calculated on the device. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings › Delete all logs, removes it.

Sync between your devices (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync, your devices exchange your logs and settings through the sync server. Each record is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is sent, using a key derived from a secret that exists only on your paired devices and in the QR code you scan to pair them. The secret never reaches the server.

The server cannot read your health data, dates, notes or settings. The server can see: a random sync space ID; a hash of the token your devices use to access it; a random ID per device; for each record an opaque ID, the time it last changed and the size of the encrypted data; and, when push is enabled, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for each device.

Push messages go through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and contain only a sequence number that tells your other devices to fetch new data. Google processes the device token and delivery information under its own privacy policy.

Web server logs

The reverse proxy in front of the server records the IP address, time and requested address of each connection for security and abuse prevention. These logs are deleted after 14 days.

Where data is kept, and for how long

The sync server runs in Portugal and its reverse proxy in Germany, both in the European Union. Synced data stays until you delete it. A sync space that no device has used for 12 months is deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Stop syncing on this device, ticking Also delete the synced data from the server, removes the space and everything in it for every device.
  • Without the app: see perixd.mildhope.net/delete.
  • On the device: Settings › Delete all logs, or uninstall Perixd.

Legal basis and your rights

Synced data is processed only because you choose to turn on sync, which is your explicit consent under Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a) of the GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time by stopping sync. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and port your data. Because synced data is encrypted and not linked to your identity, we can only act on a request that includes your Sync ID, shown in the app under Settings › Sync between devices. You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).

Health and children

Perixd shows estimates based on what you log. It is not a medical device, it is not medical advice, and it is not a method of contraception. Perixd is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
