Delete your Perixd data

Perixd has no accounts. What you log stays on your devices; the sync server only holds an encrypted copy if you turned on sync. Any of these deletes it.

In the app

Open Settings › Stop syncing on this device, tick Also delete the synced data from the server for every device, and tap Stop. The sync space and all its data are deleted at once. To also clear a device, use Settings › Delete all logs or uninstall Perixd.

With a pairing link

If you saved your pairing QR code, scan it with any QR reader to get the link that starts with perixd://pair, and paste it here. The link stays in this browser: it is only used to sign the deletion request.

By email

Email {{CONTACT}} with your Sync ID, shown in the app under Settings › Sync between devices, and the space will be deleted within 30 days. Without a Sync ID or pairing link the data cannot be linked to you, and it is deleted automatically after 12 months without use.
