## Full description (4000 max)

Perixd is a private tracker for your period, symptoms and contraception. No account, no ads, no analytics: what you log stays on your phone unless you choose to sync it, end-to-end encrypted, with your other devices.

TRACK YOUR CYCLE
• Log bleeding from spotting to heavy, with a tap from the home screen.
• Rate symptoms such as cramps, headache, back pain, bloating, tiredness and mood changes as mild, moderate or severe, and add notes.
• See your cycle day, your next period and, for regular cycles, an estimated fertile window and ovulation day. Irregular cycles get a date range instead of a guess.
• Insights show your average cycle and period length, how regular your cycles are, flow by period day, and which symptoms come back and when.

PILL, PATCH, RING, INJECTION, IUD
• Any pill pack: 21 + 7, 24 + 4, with placebo pills, continuous or progestin-only, or your own counts.
• A pill pack you can see: pills pop out as you take them, today's pill is highlighted, and once its time has passed an unmarked pill shows as out but ghosted, so you can compare it with your real pack.
• Weekly patch, monthly ring, injections every few weeks, or an IUD or implant.
• On hormonal methods Perixd hides ovulation and fertile-window estimates and follows your pack's break instead.

REMINDERS THAT DON'T GET LOST
• A daily reminder at the time you choose, with a different time on weekends if you like.
• It stays until you tap Taken, and comes back if you swipe it away.
• Optional repeat alerts, a snooze button, advance notice before a patch, ring or injection change, a heads-up before a new pack, period and fertile-window notices, and a daily reminder to log how you feel.
• Details can stay hidden on the lock screen.

YOUR DEVICES, IN SYNC (OPTIONAL)
Pair a phone and a tablet by scanning a QR code. Logs and settings are encrypted on your device before they leave it, so the sync server cannot read them. Marking a pill on one device clears the reminder on the other.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no data sale.
• Data is left out of cloud backups. You can delete all logs, or the synced copy, at any time.
• Free and open-source software (GNU AGPL 3.0).

In English and European Portuguese, with light and dark themes.

Perixd shows estimates based on what you log. It is not a medical device, not medical advice, and not a method of contraception: never rely on its predictions to prevent pregnancy. Talk to a health professional about your health and contraception.
